Crew sign-in

Smart Dustbin · Collection route

Crew sign-in is not set up yet. The owner has three steps to do once:
  1. Firebase console > Authentication > Sign-in method > enable Email/Password.
  2. Authentication > Users > Add user: the crew address from COLLECTOR_EMAIL in firebase-config.js, with the crew password. Tell the crew the password in person — never commit it.
  3. Sign in here once, then send the UID this page shows to the owner, who adds it to COLLECTOR_UIDS and to firestore.rules.

Shown in the activity log next to what you do, as “Crew - Ravi”.

The crew password is checked by Google’s servers (Firebase Authentication). This site never stores it.
Staff with a Google account can sign in on the admin login and come back here.
