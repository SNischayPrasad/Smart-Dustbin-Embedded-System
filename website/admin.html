🔬 Live firmware simulation

same thresholds as the Arduino sketch

Three sliders, three ultrasonic sensors. The first is on the front of the bin - move it under 25 cm and the lid opens. Sensors A and B sit inside the bin on opposite diagonals; their readings are averaged, so an uneven pile no longer fools the fill level. Set them far apart to see the uneven-load flag appear. The state machine, the fusion rule and the serial output are a direct port of 04_smart_dustbin_complete.ino.
